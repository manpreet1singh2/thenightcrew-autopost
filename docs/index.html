TheNightCrew

Last updated: 27 November 2026 · Contact: dimplebrar13@gmail.com

TheNightCrew runs an automation app for its own Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts. It publishes our own videos and replies to messages people send to our Pages and Instagram accounts.

Privacy Policy

What we receive. When you message one of our Facebook Pages or Instagram accounts, Meta gives our app your message, your public name or username, and an ID that Meta assigns for that conversation.

How we use it. We use this only to read your message and send you a reply. To write the reply, the recent messages of that conversation are sent to an AI text service (OpenRouter), which returns a suggested reply. We do not use your messages for advertising, and we do not sell or rent them.

What we store. We do not store message text or names. We keep only technical IDs (conversation ID and the ID of the last message we answered) so that you do not get the same reply twice. These IDs are deleted automatically over time.

Sharing. We share data only with Meta (to send the reply) and the AI text service (to write it), and when the law requires us to.

Your choices. You can stop receiving automated replies at any time by not messaging our Pages, or by asking us to delete your data (see below).

Terms of Service

Replies from our Pages and Instagram accounts may be written automatically. They are for general information and friendly conversation only. They are not an offer, promise, or professional advice. For orders, payments, complaints, or business deals, a member of our team replies personally. Please do not send passwords, OTPs, or payment details in messages. We may change these terms; the date at the top shows the latest version.

Data Deletion Instructions

To delete any data our app holds about you:

  1. Send an email to dimplebrar13@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request", or send the message "delete my data" to the Page or Instagram account you contacted.
  2. Include the name of the Page or Instagram account you messaged.
  3. We delete the stored conversation IDs within 7 days and confirm by reply.

You can also remove the conversation from your own Messenger or Instagram inbox at any time.
